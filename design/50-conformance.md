> ### CORRECTIONS (oracle-verified against Luau 0.709 — these WIN over any contradicting body row)
> 1. **`table.maxn` / `table.foreach` / `table.foreachi` / `table.getn` — PRESENT** (deprecated, analyzer-flag
>    only; no runtime effect). Oracle: all are `function`; `table.maxn({1,2,nil,4})=4`. **rustia MUST provide all
>    four** (carry deprecation metadata for the LSP). The body rows calling them "removed" are WRONG; absence is
>    the divergence.
> 2. **`getfenv` / `setfenv` — PRESENT** in the 0.709 base lib (`getfenv(0)==_G`). Provide in Standard/LuauCompat.
>    They disable environment-assuming compile-time builtin folds, but rustia's live-slot `GETIMPORT` remains
>    valid because every access starts from the current environment and revalidates shape/key before reading the
>    live value. Deterministic may capability-remove them (raising `ErrorKind::CapabilityRemoved`, never silent
>    `nil`). Delete the "absent" body rows.
> 3. **`math.map` — PRESENT** (linear remap *with extrapolation*, no clamp): `math.map(15,0,10,0,100)=150`.
>    **rustia MUST implement it.** (Resolves open-decision c7 = INCLUDE. Siblings `lerp`/`noise`/`round`/`clamp`/
>    `sign` also confirmed present.)
> 4. **`pairs`/`next` do NOT consult `__iter`** — only the generalized `for-in` STATEMENT does; `__pairs` does not
>    exist in Luau. (Fixes the row-783 vs row-281/642/782/845 internal contradiction in favour of the latter.)
> 5. **`f64`-only CONFIRMED** for 0.709 (`math.type==nil`); the "integer subtype" item is downgraded from a major
>    open decision to a **tripwire** on upstream `FFlag::LuauIntegerType2`. (See [`05-luau-target.md`](05-luau-target.md).)
> 6. **ADD: Luau does NOT guarantee proper tail calls** — `rec(200000)` stack-overflows in the oracle. rustia's
>    recursion cap must error like Luau; do **not** implement unbounded tail-call frame reuse. (Also why Wasm
>    `return_call` is irrelevant to Luau semantics.)
> 7. **`DeterministicSim` SUPPORTS weak tables.** `__mode` is honored and entries are swept at deterministic
>    instruction-count collection boundaries. The profile removes the `collectgarbage`/`gcinfo` globals and
>    `os` library, disables optional host-supplied `loadstring`, and rejects external/nondeterministic host
>    capabilities; historical rows that say weak tables themselves are forbidden are superseded.

## 2. Numbers, arithmetic, operators, coercions

| Behavior | Spec status | Real Luau behavior | rustia choice | Profile | Rationale / divergence risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single number type = IEEE-754 `f64`; no integer subtype | specified | Luau is deliberately float-only (`LUA_VECTOR`/`vector` aside); unlike Lua 5.3/5.4 there is no int/float distinction. `type(x)=="number"` for all | f64-only `Value::Number(f64)` in the 16-byte tagged Value; no fixed-point/int primitive in core Value | both | Locked (axis C). | Category | Behavior | Real Luau | rustia | Why |

| 1 | Large hex/binary integer literal overflow (e.g. 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF, 0x1_0000_0000_0000_0000) | Accumulates the literal in a 64-bit unsigned integer (wraps mod 2^64) then casts to f64; no error | Must replicate u64-wrapping accumulation then `as f64`; a naive bigint-or-error path would diverge | Observable: the resulting f64 differs if rustia saturates, errors, or accumulates differently.
