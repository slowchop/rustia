  • Same Ryzen 5 3600X host, two isolated cores (4–5), Rust 1.95. Full builds use empty target directories, control first, one sample per arm—not cold filesystem caches or forecasts for other machines.
  • Incremental results use four retained-source edit pairs, ordered AB/BA/BA/AB. Marginal medians and medians of paired savings differ. These are two sequential experiments, not one three-arm comparison; their paired speedups must not be multiplied.
  • Each simulation scene used four paired processes, 200 warmup and 2000 measured ticks. Snapshots agreed; the recorded independent review found no candidate-only defect in ten software-rendered captures per experiment.
  • Software-Vulkan frame results were mixed at roughly 2.6–2.9 FPS, not physical-GPU capacity. Capture elapsed includes deliberate waits and is not time-to-menu. Stationary captures do not establish large-world or broad gameplay performance.
  • Host-global swap activity and variable wall time remain limitations. The linked data labels GNU-time maximum process/child RSS, not aggregate concurrent memory; target footprints are per arm. Keeping both profiles consumes combined disk space.
